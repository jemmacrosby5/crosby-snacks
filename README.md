# Crosby Snacks App
🍫 A basic app for a family tradition.

Whenever someone goes on holiday they have to bring back snacks for everyone to try. The aim is to get the worst snack and the best snack, everyone will give
a rating for the country's selection out of 10 for the low and the high rating i.e. giving Norway's worst snack a score of 3/10 and its best snack 8/10.
The winning country is the score with the highest difference between low and high.

## Infrastructure
- MERN app
- Tailwind
- Deployed on Render

## Basic Initial Requirements
- Login page
- Voting page (behind auth)
- Homepage showing leaderboard
